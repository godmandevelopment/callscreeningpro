Frequently Asked Questions

Is Call Screening Pro free?

Call Screening Pro offers a 30-day free trial. After the trial, continued use requires a Google Play subscription. Current subscription options and prices are shown in the app and on Google Play before purchase.

How do I get help with a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through Google Play. To review or cancel a subscription, open Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. For app-specific questions, email support@callscreeningpro.com.

How does the app screen calls?

Call Screening Pro uses Android's call-screening functionality and your configured screening rules. Results can vary with Android version, device, and carrier.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Email support@callscreeningpro.com and include a brief description and the steps to reproduce any problem.
